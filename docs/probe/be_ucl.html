Top-Performing and Struggling Teams

The Champions League format of today has recently made a change from a group stage to a league stage. The new Champions League format sees the top eight teams in the table automatically qualify for the round of 16, teams placed 9-24 will enter a playoff round to determine if they will qualify, and teams 25-36 are eliminated from the tournament.

This can result in top teams who have recently won the tournament, like say Real Madrid or Manchester City, needing to win a two-legged playoff to make it to the knockout stage due to facing harder opponents than usual in the league stage and not performing to their highest standard.

Match Odds Overview

If you are unaware or feel like you need greater detail about the markets that have Champions League odds on our site, the below gives a description of the most popular markets:

  • 1x2 - For the 1x2 market, you bet on either the home or away team win, or for the match to end in a draw, depending on how you see the match going.

  • Over/Under - For the over/under, you will either select for there to be over a certain amount of goals, or under a certain amount for a match. For example if you selected over 1.5 goals, there would need to be a minimum of two goals in the match for it to win.

  • Both Teams To Score (BTTS) - Choose between "yes" and "no" depending on whether you think both teams will or won't score in the match. If you select "yes" and the result is 4-2, your selection will be a winning one.
