Top-Performing and Struggling Teams

Typically, the best performing teams in La Liga are Real Madrid, Barcelona, and Atletico Madrid. However, any team is capable of pulling off a surprise, and Girona proved that by finishing in third position in the 2023/24 season.

The top four teams in La Liga automatically qualify for the league stage of the Champions League, fifth place enters the Europa League, and sixth will make it to qualification for the Conference League.

Meanwhile at the bottom end of the La Liga table, teams 18th, 19th, and 20th will be relegated to La Liga 2.

Match Odds Overview

The most common market you will find with La Liga odds are described in more detail below if you are unaware of them:

  • 1x2 - Choose between betting on the home win, draw, or away win for the full-time result in any La Liga fixtures.

  • Over/Under - For the over/under market, you are selecting either more or less goals than one of the lines provided. For example, you could bet on over 2,5 goals and it would mean you need a minimum of three goals in the match from both teams combined.

  • Both Teams To Score (BTTS) - For the BTTS market you select either "yes" or "no" depending on whether you think both teams will score a minimum of one goal in the match or not.
